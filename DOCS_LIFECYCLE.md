# Well Life Cycle - 31 Stages

1.  **Site Survey & Permitting**: Geological assessment and legal approvals.
2.  **Rig Mobilization**: Moving equipment to the location.
3.  **Rig Up**: Assembling the drilling rig.
4.  **Conductor Drilling**: Drilling the first few meters for the conductor pipe.
5.  **Conductor Casing & Cementing**: Installing and cementing the conductor pipe.
6.  **Nipple Up (Diverter)**: Installing the diverter system for shallow gas.
7.  **Surface Hole Drilling**: Drilling the section for surface casing.
8.  **Surface Casing Running**: Lowering the surface casing into the well.
9.  **Surface Cementing**: Cementing the surface casing to protect aquifers.
10. **Nipple Up (BOP)**: Installing the Blowout Preventer stack.
11. **BOP Testing**: Pressure testing the safety valves.
12. **Intermediate I Hole Drilling**: Drilling through transition zones.
13. **Intermediate I Casing Running**: Installing intermediate casing.
14. **Intermediate I Cementing**: Cementing the intermediate casing.
15. **Intermediate II Hole Drilling**: Drilling towards the target reservoir.
16. **Intermediate II Casing Running**: Installing second intermediate casing.
17. **Intermediate II Cementing**: Cementing the second intermediate casing.
18. **Production Hole Drilling**: Drilling the final section into the reservoir.
19. **Open Hole Logging**: Running sensors to evaluate the reservoir.
20. **Production Casing / Liner Running**: Final pipe string before production.
21. **Production Cementing**: Ensuring zonal isolation.
22. **Well Completion**: Installing tubing, packers, and safety valves.
23. **Nipple Down BOP / Nipple Up Tree**: Removing BOP and installing the Christmas Tree.
24. **Perforation**: Blowing holes in the casing to allow oil/gas flow.
25. **Stimulation**: Acidizing or hydraulic fracturing to increase flow.
26. **Flowback & Testing**: Cleaning the well and measuring initial flow.
27. **Production Operations**: Steady-state extraction of oil and gas.
28. **Workover / Intervention**: Maintenance and repair of downhole equipment.
29. **Enhanced Oil Recovery**: Injecting water/gas to maintain pressure.
30. **Plug & Abandonment (Temporary)**: Suspending operations safely.
31. **Final Decommissioning**: Permanent plugging and site restoration.
